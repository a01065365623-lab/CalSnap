3. How We Use Information

  • Food photos and entered text: used only to show you the estimated food type, calories, and nutrients.
  • Temporary user ID: used only to enforce the per-device daily recognition limit (abuse prevention); never used for advertising, marketing, or identifying you.
  • Advertising ID, crash/diagnostic information, and app interactions: used by Google AdMob for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and error analysis.
  • On-device logs: used on your device only, for daily/period calorie, nutrient, exercise, and weight statistics and calorie targets.
  • We do not sell your information and do not share it with third parties except as described in Section 4.

5. Data Security

  • Encryption in transit: all traffic between the App and our server, and between our server and the Google Gemini API, is encrypted with HTTPS.
  • No storage of originals: food photos and text you enter are handled in server memory only during recognition and are never written to our server's disk or database.
  • Hashing and automatic deletion: the temporary user ID is stored on our server only as an irreversible hash, together with daily usage counts, and is automatically deleted after 7 days. For older app versions that do not send the ID, a hash of the IP address is used in the same way.
  • Access control: only the Developer can access the server, database, and API keys, and the hosting account is protected by authentication.
  • On-device protection: the App Lock (Secret Mode) PIN is never stored in plain text; it is hashed with SHA-256 and a per-device random salt.

6. Retention and Deletion

  • On-device records: meal, exercise, weight, and water logs and body information are stored only on your device and kept until you delete them. You can delete records by date, or use Reset Profile / Full Reset in Settings; uninstalling the App removes all local data.
  • Server data: food photos and entered text are not stored. The hashed temporary user ID and daily usage counts are deleted automatically after 7 days. The recognition result cache (food names, calories, etc., not linked to you) lives only in server memory and is cleared when the server restarts.
  • Hosting access logs: request times, IP addresses, and similar records in Railway's logs are used only for troubleshooting and abuse prevention and are deleted when the hosting provider's retention period ends.
  • Advertising data: data collected by Google AdMob is managed under Google's policies; you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings or at Google My Ad Center.
  • Deletion requests: for any other deletion request or question, email teeth07@naver.com. See the data deletion instructions for details.

7. Health-Related Information

Your weight, exercise, and meal (including water) logs, body information (sex, age, height, weight), and photos attached to weight logs are stored only in your device's local storage and are never sent to our server. The Developer has no access to this information. (Food photos and food names sent when you request recognition are used only for recognition and are not stored, as described in Section 2.)

  • Android: Settings > Privacy (or Google) > Ads > Delete advertising ID
  • iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking > turn off "Allow Apps to Request to Track"
